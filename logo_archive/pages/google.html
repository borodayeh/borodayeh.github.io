Historical Evolution

1998: First Logo

The original Google logo was created by Sergey Brin using GIMP. It featured a simple wordmark with a green 'G' and red 'o'.

1999: Ruth Kedar Design

Professional designer Ruth Kedar created a new logo with the now-famous primary colors and playful typography.

2015: Current Logo

Google introduced a new, flatter design that maintains the playful character while being more modern and scalable.

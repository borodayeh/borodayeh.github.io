Nike

1971 - Present Carolyn Davidson

The Nike Swoosh is one of the most recognizable brand symbols in the world. Designed by Carolyn Davidson in 1971, it represents motion and speed, embodying the Greek goddess Nike's spirit of victory.

Historical Evolution

1971: Creation of the Swoosh

Carolyn Davidson designed the iconic Swoosh logo for just $35, creating one of the most recognizable brand symbols in history.

1978: Swoosh Standalone

Nike began using the Swoosh without the wordmark, establishing it as a powerful standalone symbol.

1995: Wordmark Redesign

The Nike wordmark was updated while keeping the Swoosh unchanged, creating a more modern and cohesive brand identity.
